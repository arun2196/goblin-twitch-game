# Gobbo Games Season 3 Finale

## The Search for the Imperial Bonker

### Event Purpose

The Season 3 finale concludes with the crowning of the new Gobbo Emperor.

Before the coronation can take place, an ancient requirement must be fulfilled. The future Emperor must possess the Imperial Bonker, an ancient relic of the Gobbo Empire that disappeared beneath the ocean 114 years ago.

The finale follows the Gobbos as they launch an expedition to recover the Bonker.

The event should prioritize:

* Hype and spectacle
* Frequent viewer participation
* Handcrafted narration
* Comedy and escalating Gobbo incompetence
* The illusion that viewer decisions significantly affect the expedition
* Callbacks to earlier decisions
* A gradual transition from comedy into a more ceremonial ending
* The coronation as the true conclusion of the season

Most choices should affect narration rather than the final route. The expedition ultimately reaches the Imperial Bonker regardless of individual successes, failures, or decisions.

---

# EVENT FLOW

## PROLOGUE - THE CORONATION

The Gobbo Games are over.

A winner has emerged and is ready to become the next Gobbo Emperor.

The crown is prepared.

The Gobbos are assembled.

There is, however, one problem.

Ancient Gobbo law states that an Emperor cannot be crowned without bearing the Imperial Bonker.

The Imperial Bonker was the ceremonial weapon of the old Gobbo Emperors.

It has been missing for 114 years.

Historical records state that the Bonker was lost at sea.

Recently discovered records have identified the approximate location where it disappeared.

The coronation therefore cannot proceed.

An Imperial Expedition is declared.

Objective:

RECOVER THE IMPERIAL BONKER.

---

# ENCOUNTER 1 - THE EXPEDITION ASSEMBLES

Purpose:

Introduce the event, establish the objective and allow everyone to participate immediately.

Interaction:

Players join the Imperial Expedition.

Possible command:

!join

The number of participating Gobbos should be incorporated into the narration.

Example structure:

"The call goes out across the Gobbo Empire."

"[X] Gobbos answer."

"Supplies are gathered. Weapons are loaded. Maps are consulted."

"The Imperial Expedition sets sail."

No meaningful possibility of failure exists during this stage.

This is primarily the opening ceremony of the finale.

---

# ENCOUNTER 2 - TROUBLE AT SEA

The expedition has been sailing toward the last known location of the Imperial Bonker.

Something intercepts the Gobbo vessel.

Exact enemy can be decided later.

Possible concepts:

* Pirates
* Rival treasure hunters
* Imperial Bonker cultists
* Descendants of the original ship's crew
* A naval faction attempting to prevent anyone from reaching the location

This is the first combat encounter.

Players participate using the normal encounter system.

### Success

The attackers are defeated.

The Gobbos continue toward the coordinates.

Before the enemy disappears, something strange is discovered.

They were not attempting to rob the Gobbos.

They were trying to prevent them from reaching their destination.

This introduces the first indication that the official history of the Imperial Bonker may be incomplete.

### Failure

The ship suffers damage.

The encounter reopens for reinforcements.

Each failure escalates the damage and absurdity.

Attempt 1:
Normal naval battle.

Attempt 2:
Parts of the ship are damaged.

Attempt 3:
Important equipment begins disappearing or catching fire.

Attempt 4:
The ship is barely functioning.

Attempt 5:
Victory is effectively guaranteed.

The expedition continues regardless.

---

# ENCOUNTER 3 - THE WATER SEAGULLS

The ship reaches the coordinates where the Imperial Bonker supposedly disappeared 114 years ago.

The sea becomes unusually quiet.

The Gobbos prepare to begin their search.

They are suddenly attacked by Water Seagulls.

Water Seagulls are not explained.

They should be treated by the narration as though their existence is completely normal.

This encounter introduces the first major viewer vote.

## DECISION

OPTION 1:
Fight the Water Seagulls.

Command:

!option1

OPTION 2:
Feed the Water Seagulls.

Command:

!option2

Votes remain open for a limited period.

The winning choice determines the narration.

### OPTION 1 WINS

The Gobbos fight the Water Seagulls.

Combat or a group roll resolves the encounter.

The Water Seagulls are eventually driven away.

Store:

water_seagulls = hostile

This can affect narration during a later encounter.

### OPTION 2 WINS

The Gobbos throw food overboard.

The Water Seagulls stop attacking and consume the expedition's supplies.

The expedition is now several hours from home with significantly less food.

Store:

water_seagulls = fed

This can affect narration during a later encounter.

### FIXED RESULT

The Water Seagulls are removed from the immediate area.

The expedition proceeds.

---

# ENCOUNTER 4 - DESCENT

The Gobbos search the ocean surface.

There is no wreck.

There is no visible Imperial Bonker.

There is nothing indicating that a ship sank here.

The expedition therefore deploys its submarines.

The submarines should be described as individually questionable Gobbo engineering projects.

Possible details can be randomly inserted into narration:

* One submarine has pedals.
* One appears to be a barrel.
* One has been assembled backwards.
* One Gobbo has brought a diving bell.
* One vessel appears to be a bathtub with modifications.
* Someone forgot to install a window.
* Someone installed several unnecessary windows.

The Gobbos descend.

This section can contain a small group roll or minor interaction, but should primarily function as narration and transition.

During the descent, various submarine accidents and shenanigans occur.

Eventually the expedition reaches the ocean floor.

---

# ENCOUNTER 5 - THE ROAD

The Gobbos expected to find wreckage.

Instead they discover an ancient stone road.

The road appears to have been constructed long before the Imperial Bonker disappeared.

It leads away from the supposed wreck location.

The expedition follows it.

Ruins begin appearing.

Statues.

Broken structures.

Ancient Gobbo markings.

Eventually the road reaches an enormous sealed entrance.

The door contains an inscription and a riddle.

---

# ENCOUNTER 6 - THE RIDDLE

This encounter uses free-text answers.

Command:

!answer [answer]

A riddle appears on the ancient door.

Example placeholder riddle:

"I belong to you,
yet other Gobbos use me more than you do.
What am I?"

Answer:

Your name.

The exact riddle should be finalized separately.

Players have several minutes to submit answers.

Answers should not immediately reveal whether they are correct.

The system records correct answers.

Periodic narration can report participation.

Example:

"Twenty-seven answers have now been shouted at the door."

"The door remains unmoved."

"Someone has answered 'gold' for the fourth time."

After the timer expires, answers close.

The correct answer is revealed.

Players who answered correctly can be named.

Optional:

Correct answers receive a small gold reward.

The door opens.

---

# ENCOUNTER 7 - THE TRUTH ABOUT THE IMPERIAL BONKER

The expedition enters the structure.

Inside are preserved records from the old Gobbo Empire.

The Gobbos begin translating them.

The official history is wrong.

The Imperial Bonker was not accidentally lost at sea 114 years ago.

It was deliberately placed beneath the ocean.

The last Gobbo Emperor ordered it hidden.

The underwater structure was constructed or repurposed specifically to protect it.

The location is not a tomb.

It is a vault.

The previous Emperor believed that possession of the Imperial Bonker should not automatically pass to whoever inherited power.

Anyone attempting to recover it would first have to prove themselves capable of leading Gobbos.

The expedition has unknowingly entered an ancient succession trial.

Possible inscription:

"IF YOU HAVE COME TO CLAIM THE IMPERIAL BONKER, YOU MUST FIRST PROVE YOURSELF CAPABLE OF LEADING GOBBOS."

Additional smaller inscription:

"OUR CONDOLENCES."

---

# ENCOUNTER 8 - THE TWO PATHS

The expedition encounters a major fork within the vault.

This creates another apparent decision.

## OPTION 1 - THE PATH OF WISDOM

A carefully constructed route involving puzzles, mechanisms and ancient traps.

Command:

!option1

## OPTION 2 - THE PATH OF CHAOS

An obviously questionable alternative route.

This may simply be a damaged wall, suspicious tunnel, enormous hole or other clearly unintended entrance.

Command:

!option2

The community votes.

### OPTION 1 WINS

The Gobbos attempt the Path of Wisdom.

They overcome puzzles and traps.

After considerable effort they reach the next chamber.

A section of the wall then collapses.

The Path of Chaos is revealed to have led directly to the same location.

Store:

vault_path = wisdom

### OPTION 2 WINS

The Gobbos ignore the elaborate ancient trial and force their way through the alternative route.

They arrive directly in the next chamber.

From the other side they can see the enormous series of traps and puzzles they bypassed.

Store:

vault_path = chaos

### FIXED RESULT

Both choices reach the same chamber.

The chosen route can be referenced during later narration.

---

# ENCOUNTER 9 - FINAL TRIAL

The Gobbos enter the deepest section of the vault.

The Imperial Bonker is close.

Something prevents them from reaching it.

Exact nature of the final opponent should be finalized separately.

Potential direction:

An ancient Guardian created or appointed by the previous Emperor.

This should be the largest traditional encounter of the finale.

All available Gobbos can participate.

Gobbo Cards can contribute.

The encounter supports repeated attempts and reinforcements.

### ATTEMPT STRUCTURE

Attempt 1:

The Guardian is encountered at full strength.

Narration remains relatively serious.

Attempt 2:

Reinforcements arrive.

The battlefield begins taking damage.

Attempt 3:

The ancient vault begins breaking apart.

Earlier choices can begin returning as callbacks.

Attempt 4:

The situation becomes increasingly chaotic.

Attempt 5:

Victory is effectively guaranteed.

### WATER SEAGULL CALLBACK

If:

water_seagulls = fed

The Water Seagulls may return during the battle and unexpectedly assist or interfere in a beneficial way.

If:

water_seagulls = hostile

The Water Seagulls may return angry and attack everything in sight, inadvertently affecting the battle.

Both routes ultimately provide different narration around the same mechanical assistance or event.

### VAULT PATH CALLBACK

The selected Wisdom/Chaos route can also affect how reinforcements arrive or how part of the battlefield collapses.

The objective is to make earlier choices appear consequential without creating separate finale branches.

# ENCOUNTER 10 - THE IMPERIAL BONKER

The Guardian is defeated.

Combat ends.

The narration slows down.

The expedition enters the final chamber.

The Imperial Bonker is revealed.

It has remained beneath the ocean for 114 years.

The future Emperor approaches the relic.

The Bonker cannot initially be removed.

An inscription reveals an apparent contradiction:

"ONLY THE EMPEROR MAY WIELD THE IMPERIAL BONKER."

The Gobbos have a problem.

The candidate requires the Imperial Bonker to complete the coronation.

However, the Imperial Bonker apparently requires them to already be Emperor.

The expedition has discovered a 114-year-old Gobbo constitutional problem.

A final portion of the inscription is discovered.

Exact wording can be refined later.

Core meaning:

The Imperial Bonker does not decide who becomes Emperor.

The Gobbos do.

---

# FINAL INTERACTION - HAIL THE EMPEROR

The candidate stands before the Imperial Bonker.

All viewers are invited to proclaim their support.

Command:

!hail

Unlike previous interactions:

There is no opposing option.

There is no combat roll.

There is no failure state.

The purpose is purely ceremonial and communal.

The system records unique participants.

During the window, narration can periodically announce the growing number.

Example:

"12 Gobbos have spoken."

"26 Gobbos now hail [NAME]."

"41 voices echo through the ancient vault."

After the participation window closes, the final number is announced.

The candidate takes hold of the Imperial Bonker.

The relic releases.

The candidate raises the Imperial Bonker.

The ancient succession trial is complete.

---

# RETURN

The expedition must now return to the Gobbo Empire.

This section should be deliberately shorter than the journey outward.

Potential joke:

After the enormous expedition, ship battles, Water Seagulls, submarines, ruins and ancient trials, somebody discovers that the Gobbos had access to a much easier method of returning home.

The expedition returns.

The coronation resumes.

---

# THE CORONATION

This is the true climax of the Season 3 finale.

There should be no additional surprise boss.

There should be no further failure state.

The event should be allowed to slow down.

The Gobbos assemble.

The future Emperor enters carrying the recovered Imperial Bonker.

The narration recalls major events from the expedition.

Possible callbacks:

* Number of Gobbos who originally joined.
* Ship battle participants.
* Water Seagull decision.
* Riddle solvers.
* Wisdom/Chaos vote.
* Notable combat performances.
* Number of attempts required to defeat the final Guardian.
* Particularly funny failures.
* Number of Gobbos who used !hail.

The candidate kneels.

The crown, which has remained waiting at home throughout the entire expedition, is finally presented.

The candidate is crowned.

The Imperial Bonker is raised.

The winner of Gobbo Games Season 3 is formally declared:

GOBBO EMPEROR.

The season ends with the coronation rather than the final battle.

SEASON 3 ENDS

# DESIGN PRINCIPLE: ILLUSION OF CHOICE

The event should appear more branching than it actually is.

Viewer decisions primarily modify:

* Narration
* Stored flags
* Later callbacks
* Character reactions
* Encounter descriptions
* Minor bonuses or penalties
* Which jokes become available

They should generally not modify:

* Major encounter order
* Availability of the Imperial Bonker
* Whether the expedition can finish
* Whether the winner can be crowned

Every major path eventually converges.

This allows handcrafted narration for meaningful-looking decisions without requiring multiple complete finale stories.

The important rule is that choices should be remembered.

A decision that appears cosmetic when it happens can become meaningful later through a callback.

This creates the impression that the story is responding continuously to the community.

---

# INTERACTION TYPES

The finale currently uses four primary interaction systems.

## 1. PARTICIPATION

Used for battles and large encounters.

Example:

!join

Purpose:

Create scale and allow Gobbo Cards/player stats to contribute.

## 2. COMMUNITY DECISION

Commands:

!option1

!option2

Purpose:

Allow chat to collectively determine how the Gobbos respond to a situation.

Usually changes narration rather than destination.

## 3. RIDDLE

Command:

!answer [response]

Purpose:

Individual participation.

Allows viewers to genuinely solve something rather than simply vote.

Correct answers can be tracked and rewarded.

## 4. CEREMONIAL PARTICIPATION

Command:

!hail

Purpose:

Final community moment.

No competition.

No incorrect choice.

No failure.

Every participant contributes to the final coronation.

2. Exact riddle or riddles used in the underwater vault.

3. Additional submarine encounter or interaction during the descent.

4. Exact nature and history of the final Guardian.

5. Whether the Guardian knows the candidate's identity and directly addresses them.

6. Whether Gobbo Cards have special finale-specific interactions.

7. Exact explanation for why the Imperial Bonker was hidden 114 years ago.

8. Whether the "Only the Emperor may wield the Bonker" paradox remains the final twist or is replaced with a stronger reveal.

9. How previous Season 3 events or characters can appear as callbacks without overwhelming the finale.

10. Exact coronation narration and final Season 3 closing line.

The story structure should be finalized before encounter probabilities and implementation details are designed.
